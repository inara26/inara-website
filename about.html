About the studio

Spaces shaped by light, balance and purpose.

To us, architecture is an act of illumination, not just of space but of spirit. Every project is a dialogue between light and material, human and nature, idea and impact. At Inara Architects, we design spaces that radiate purpose, clarity and emotion, leaving an impression that lingers beyond walls.

Our story

A young practice with a clear vision.

Founded on 10 June 2024, Inara Architects began with a clear vision: to design meaningful spaces shaped by light, balance and purpose. From a small start, we steadily grew into a practice handling diverse projects in residential, interior, commercial and hospital design, taking responsibility for both concept development and execution. Each project strengthened our commitment to precision, creativity and client trust. Today, Inara Architects continues to evolve as a young, dedicated firm, creating spaces that inspire, function beautifully and stand the test of time.

The name Inara means a ray of light. It is a reminder of what we value most in a building: the way light enters it, moves through it and changes how it feels across a day.

Vision

Where we are going

To create environmentally responsible designs that harmonise with nature and foster a strong, meaningful connection between people and the spaces they inhabit.

Mission

How we get there

To create functional, sustainable and human-centred spaces through thoughtful design and precise execution.

Core values

Four ideas behind every drawing.

Creativity

Every brief starts on a blank sheet. We look for the idea that belongs to this site, this family, this business.

Integrity

Clear drawings, clear costs and clear timelines. We tell you what a decision means before you make it.

Excellence

Detail is where a design is won or lost. We draw it, check it on site, and draw it again if we must.

Collaboration

Clients, consultants and craftspeople sit at one table. Good buildings come from good conversations.

Milestones

From a small start to a growing practice.

Two years, 29 projects in our portfolio, and work across 20+ towns and cities in Tamil Nadu.

June 2024

Inara is founded

Ar. Bharath Munusamy opens the studio in Moulivakkam with a focus on residential architecture and interiors.

2024 to 2025

Homes across Tamil Nadu

Residences in Chennai, Mannargudi, Chinnasalem, Tindivanam and beyond. Complete-home interiors in Vanagaram, Maduravoyal and Coimbatore.

2025

Commercial and healthcare

Showrooms in Kallakurichi, a banquet hall in Ayyapanthangal, offices for SM Land Developers, and interiors for the Tata Cancer Hospital.

2025 to today

The public realm

Redevelopment studies for T. Nagar and Kotturpuram Park extend the studio's work from homes to the city.
